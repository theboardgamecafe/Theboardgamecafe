Privacy notice

How we use
your information.

This notice explains how The Board Game Café handles information you provide through this website, email, phone or WhatsApp, including when you sign up for marketing emails or WhatsApp messages.

Who is responsible for your information?

THE BOARD GAME CAFE LTD (company number 16480680), trading as The Board Game Café at Unit 12, The Stables, Eanam, Blackburn BB1 5BY, is the data controller for booking, enquiry and marketing signup information. You can contact us at bookings@theboardgamecafe.co.uk or 07768 489048.

What information we collect

Depending on what you ask us to do, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, preferred date and time, party or group size, event details, accessibility information you choose to share, and the contents of your message.

Why we use it and our lawful basis

We use booking and enquiry information to answer your request, check availability, discuss options, prepare a booking or event proposal, and manage any resulting booking. Where you ask us to take steps before making a booking, we process the information because it is necessary to take those steps at your request. We may also rely on our legitimate interests to keep practical correspondence, prevent misuse of the website and maintain appropriate business records, and on legal obligations where records must be retained by law.

Who processes the information

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Website form submissions are transmitted through Web3Forms to our booking inbox. Web3Forms acts as a processor for form submissions and states that its infrastructure includes Amazon Web Services, Cloudflare and Hetzner. Its current privacy policy says submission data can be retained for up to three years unless a shorter retention period is configured or the data is deleted earlier, and that international transfers from the UK may rely on safeguards including Standard Contractual Clauses. Web3Forms also states that its spam-prevention services may process submitter IP and email information through providers such as CleanTalk and Akismet. You can read the Web3Forms Privacy Policy. Email is handled through our business email provider. If you choose WhatsApp, your message is processed through WhatsApp.

How long we keep it

We keep our own enquiry information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the enquiry, booking, customer service, security and business-record purpose for which it was collected. Enquiries that do not lead to a booking are reviewed and deleted when no longer needed. Booking and transaction records may need to be retained for longer where accounting, tax, legal or dispute-resolution requirements apply. Separately, Web3Forms states that form-submission data may be retained on its service for up to three years unless a shorter retention setting applies or the data is deleted earlier.

Marketing choices

You can choose to receive occasional offers, café news and event invitations by email, WhatsApp or both. We rely on separate consent for each channel. Signup is optional and separate from booking or visiting the café. We record when and how you signed up, what you agreed to and any withdrawal of consent for each channel.

Email marketing

When you opt in by email, we use your email address and consent checkbox to send your welcome offer and future marketing emails. Brevo hosts our signup form, stores email subscription records and sends emails on our behalf. Use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email to stop email marketing.

WhatsApp marketing

When you opt in through WhatsApp Business, we use your mobile number and JOIN message to send your welcome offer and future marketing messages. WhatsApp processes messages and related information through its service. Reply STOP on WhatsApp to stop WhatsApp marketing.

Each opt-out applies to its own channel. You can also contact us at bookings@theboardgamecafe.co.uk to withdraw consent for either or both channels. You have the right to object to direct marketing. We review these records periodically, remove records when they are no longer needed and may keep the minimum record needed to honour your opt-out. Where information is transferred outside the UK, applicable safeguards may include UK adequacy regulations or approved contract terms. Contact us for details.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights to access your information, correct it, ask for deletion, restrict how it is used, object to certain processing, or receive information in a portable form. You can contact us using the details above to make a request.

Complaints

If you have a concern, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Form security

Our forms use a spam-trap field and are intended only for genuine booking and enquiry messages. Do not submit sensitive information that is not necessary for us to deal with your request.

Cookies and browser storage

Optional website statistics are described below. No advertising service is loaded. It uses temporary browser session storage to carry contact details between the enquiry and booking forms and to recognise a successful form submission. These temporary values are not a separate customer database. The café’s font files are hosted with the website. Some images are loaded from external providers. Links to Google Maps, social media and WhatsApp take you to those providers, which apply their own privacy and cookie policies.

Optional website statistics

Optional statistics start off. Choose “Allow cookies” in the Cookie settings to enable them. Choose “Reject optional cookies” at any time in the footer’s Cookie settings to keep statistics off or withdraw your choice. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control preferences keep statistics off.

If enabled, the website sends the page path and a fixed event name, such as a page view, contact-button click or successful enquiry. Names, email addresses, phone numbers, form text, URL query strings, advertising IDs and visitor identifiers are not included in that statistics payload. The service stores daily aggregate counts, rather than individual visitor histories. Counts are not unique visitor numbers and reflect only people who enable statistics.

Statistics are processed through our website service hosted on OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare. Hosting providers also handle technical requests needed to deliver and protect the service. Aggregate reports cover up to 180 days; expired rows are removed in bounded cleanup batches as new statistics arrive. A simple on/off choice is saved in this browser’s local storage. No advertising cookies are used by this statistics feature.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our website, suppliers or data-handling processes change. The version published on this page is the current notice.
