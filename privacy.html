Privacy notice

How we use
enquiry information.

This short notice explains what happens when you contact us through this website.

Information you provide

When you submit an enquiry, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, preferred date, group size and information about your booking or event.

Why we use it

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, discuss availability, prepare a booking or event proposal, and keep a practical record of correspondence.

How the form is processed

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Website form submissions are transmitted through Web3Forms to The Board Game Café’s booking inbox. GitHub Pages does not store the form entries in the website itself. Web3Forms processes submission data to deliver the enquiry and may retain it in line with its own service and privacy terms. You can read the Web3Forms privacy policy. If you choose WhatsApp instead, your message is sent through WhatsApp.

How long information is kept

We keep enquiry information only as long as it is reasonably needed for the booking, correspondence, business records or any legal obligations that apply.

Your choices

You can contact us if you want us to correct or delete enquiry information that we hold, subject to any legal or business record-keeping requirements.
