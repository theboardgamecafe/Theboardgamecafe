Plan your game

Choose the pace of your match

A slower game leaves time to think. If you’d like several rounds in one visit, agree a time control with your opponent before you start.

Play one against one

Chess is a two-player game. If four of you visit together, take turns at one board or ask what’s available for two games.

Teach a first game

Start by showing how each piece moves. Once your opponent is comfortable with turns, explain check, checkmate and how a draw can happen.

Practise a simple opening

Try bringing knights and bishops into play and contesting the centre early. After the game, pick one turning point to review together.
