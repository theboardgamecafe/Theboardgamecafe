About the café

A place to
spend time together.

The Board Game Café is an independent, family-friendly social venue built around games, desserts, conversation and giving friends, families and groups somewhere different to spend time together.

What we’re building

Less formality.
More reasons to stay.

The idea is deliberately broader than a traditional board-game café.

Social first

Games are there to help people connect, not to make the venue feel exclusive to hobbyists.

More than one activity

Board games, consoles, screens, sport, movie nights, desserts and events all sit under one roof.

Family friendly

Families and children are welcome, with games, consoles, desserts and relaxed seating giving different ages something to enjoy.
