About The Board Game Café

A place to
spend time together.

The Board Game Café is an independent social gaming café and lounge in Blackburn. We’ve built it for people who want more from a night out than simply sitting at a table — somewhere to play, watch, eat, compete, celebrate and actually spend time together.

Plan a visitExplore the café
Why we exist

Connection first.
Games make it easier.

The strongest gaming cafés and social venues have one thing in common: the activity is a reason for people to connect. That is the idea here too. Board games are part of it, but so are console sessions, live sport, movie nights, desserts, karak chai, group events and private celebrations.

You do not need to be a serious gamer. You just need a reason to get together.

What makes us different
