Plan your game

One round or a rematch

Allow around 20 to 40 minutes for a game, depending on the group and the rolls. Ludo works as a first game of the visit before you switch to something longer.

Get everyone started

Pick one colour each and explain how a piece leaves its starting area. Check that everyone knows where their home lane is before the first roll.

Agree your house rules

Ludo sets and families can differ on what a six does or which squares are safe. A quick agreement at the start keeps turns moving.

Choose when to race or catch

You can often choose between moving a piece towards home and trying to land on an opponent’s piece. Look at who’s close behind before choosing.

Before you visit

Ludo questions

Find us at Unit 12, The Stables, Eanam, Blackburn BB1 5BY. Check the opening hours and directions before you set off.

Is Ludo suitable for younger players?

It’s a useful first board game for children who can count spaces and take turns. An adult can help with the starting and home-lane rules.

Can all four of us play?

Yes. Our board-game tables seat up to four and Ludo supports two to four players.
