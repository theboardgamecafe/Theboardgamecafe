How to join

Signup is for guests aged 18 or over. Confirm each channel you choose.

  • Email. Open the email form, enter your email address and tick the consent box.
  • WhatsApp. Open WhatsApp and send the prefilled JOIN message.
  • Both. Complete the email form and send the WhatsApp message to opt in to both channels.

Joining is optional and separate from any booking. Unsubscribe from an email or reply STOP on WhatsApp at any time. Read our marketing privacy notice.
