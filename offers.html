How to join

Signup is for guests aged 18 or over. Choose whichever option suits you.

  • Email. Open the email form, enter your email address and tick the consent box. We’ll send the welcome offer to your inbox.
  • WhatsApp. Open WhatsApp and send the prefilled JOIN message. We’ll reply there with the welcome offer.
  • Both. Complete the email form and send the WhatsApp message to opt in to both channels.

Joining is optional and separate from any booking. Unsubscribe from an email or reply STOP on WhatsApp at any time. Read our marketing privacy notice.

Welcome offer terms

Get a free karak chai when you buy a full-priced dessert. Use it on any day we’re open within 14 days of receiving your code. One offer per transaction. It can’t be combined with other offers. Show the offer code from our email or WhatsApp reply before you pay.
