Guests & timing

Tell us your guest numbers and preferred layout so we can confirm a suitable capacity. Exclusive whole-venue hire is £100 per hour, with a three-hour minimum (£300). You can request any day or time, subject to availability and confirmation.

Food & refreshments

Catering can be arranged; food and refreshments are charged separately from venue hire. You may bring outside food for a £50 fee. Guest kitchen use is available; agree your plans with us before booking. No VAT is added by the café.

Parking & access

Free parking on site, including accessible parking. Step-free street-level entrance and wheelchair access. There is no accessible toilet; tell us about any access requirements before booking.

The venue is alcohol-free. Free Wi-Fi, board games, PS5 consoles and TVs are available. Tell us the date, start time, duration, guest numbers and any catering or screen requirements in your enquiry.
