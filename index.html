How much does a table cost?

Your first hour is £15 per table, including four drinks. Each additional hour is £8 per table. The planner above shows the estimate for your visit.

Can I come on my own?

Yes. Check upcoming open games or ask the café about finding another player.

Can I bring the family?

Families and children are welcome. Ask us about your preferred time if you’re planning a late visit with younger children.
