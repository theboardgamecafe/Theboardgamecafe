Quick answers

Before you come.

How much does a table cost?

The first hour is £15 per table and includes four drinks. Each additional hour is £8 per table.

Do I need to be a serious board gamer?

No. Come for games, consoles, desserts, sport, movie nights or simply a catch-up.

Can I book a birthday or private event?

Yes. Birthday parties, group bookings and private venue hire are available.

Is the café family friendly?

Yes. Families and children are welcome. For later evening visits with younger children, contact us if you want to check a particular time or event.
