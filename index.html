Quick answers

Before you come.

Everything you’re most likely to want to know before heading over.

How much does a table cost?

The first hour is £15 per table and includes four drinks. Each additional hour is £8 per table.

Do I need to be a serious board gamer?

No. The café is designed for casual catch-ups, console sessions, desserts, live sport, movie nights and group events as well as board games.

Can I book a birthday party or private event?

Yes. Birthday parties, group bookings and private venue hire are all available.

Can I come just for desserts or karak chai?

Yes. You are welcome to visit for drinks and desserts as well as gaming.

Do you show live sport?

Selected live sporting events are shown. Message us to confirm a particular fixture.
