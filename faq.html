Frequently asked questions

How much does a table cost?

The first hour is £15 per table and includes four drinks. Each additional hour is £8 per table.

Do I need to book?

Booking ahead is recommended for busy evenings, larger groups and events. For a quick table enquiry, message us on WhatsApp.

Do I need to be a serious board gamer?

No. The café is designed for casual catch-ups, console gaming, desserts, live sport, movie nights and group events as well as board games.

Can I visit just for desserts or karak chai?

Yes. You are welcome to visit for drinks, desserts and a relaxed catch-up even if you are not planning a full gaming session.

What games do you have?

The venue has a selection of familiar board games and social favourites, with console gaming and tournament-style activities also available.

Do you have console gaming?

Yes. Console gaming is part of the venue experience, with lounge seating and large screens.

Do you show live sport?

Selected live sporting events are shown on the venue screens. Message us to confirm a particular fixture before travelling.

Do you run movie nights?

Yes. We offer selected movie-night style events and can discuss suitable private group viewing setups.

Can I book a birthday party?

Yes. Birthday parties can be arranged as group bookings or, where suitable, private venue hire.

Can I hire the whole venue?

Yes. Private hire is available for celebrations, businesses, schools, charities, clubs, community groups and other organised events.

Can businesses or schools book?

Yes. Corporate socials, workshops, school and college group visits and organised activities can all be discussed with us.

Is there parking?

Free parking is available around The Stables and nearby. Follow local signage and any restrictions in place when you visit.

Is the venue wheelchair accessible?

The venue is currently listed with wheelchair-accessible parking, entrance, seating and toilet facilities. Contact us if you have a specific access requirement.

Are children welcome?

Yes. The café is family friendly. For late-evening visits with younger children, contact us if you would like to check suitability for a particular time or event.

Do you serve alcohol?

No. The café is alcohol-free and serves hot drinks, cold drinks, mocktails, milkshakes and other non-alcoholic options.

Do you have Wi-Fi?

Yes. Free Wi-Fi is available for customers.

Can you arrange catering for groups?

Yes. Pre-arranged catering and group food/drink options can be discussed when you enquire.

Can we use the TVs or projector for an event?

For suitable group and private bookings, ask us about using venue screens for presentations, branded content or event material.

How do I contact you?

Call or WhatsApp 07768 489048, email bookings@theboardgamecafe.co.uk, or use the enquiry form on our Contact page.
