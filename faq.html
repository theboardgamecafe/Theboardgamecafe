Frequently asked questions

Everything you need
before you visit.

Pricing, bookings, games, food, group events and practical details — without making you hunt through the site.

Visiting & playing

How much does a table cost?

Tables are £8 an hour per table, not per person. Drinks and food are ordered separately from the menu.

Do I need to book?

No. Walk-ins are welcome. Booking ahead is recommended for busy evenings, larger groups and events. For a same-day visit, call or WhatsApp 07768 489048 to check availability.

Do I need to be a serious board gamer?

No. The café is designed for casual catch-ups, console gaming, desserts, live sport, movie nights and group events as well as board games.

What games do you have?

Browse board games and PS5 options. Ask us to confirm a particular game or setup before visiting.

Do you have console gaming?

Yes. Console gaming is part of the venue experience, with lounge seating and large screens.

Can I watch Champions League games?

Our TNT Sports commercial package includes UEFA Champions League coverage and Amazon Prime’s Tuesday picks. We’re currently closed on Tuesdays; Tuesday match nights are coming soon. Contact us to confirm your fixture and screen before travelling.

Do you run movie nights?

Movie-night requests are subject to confirmation of the film, screening permissions and a suitable setup. Contact us with your preferred film, date and group size before making plans.

Can I visit just for desserts or karak chai?

Yes. You are welcome to visit for drinks, desserts and a relaxed catch-up even if you are not planning a full gaming session.

Is the food halal?

Yes. All the food we serve is halal.

Do you serve alcohol?

No. The café is alcohol-free and serves hot drinks, cold drinks, mocktails, milkshakes and other non-alcoholic options.

When is my table confirmed?

Tables for up to four guests are confirmed straight away when you book online, and you pay at the café when you arrive. For bigger groups, parties and private hire, we reply to confirm availability and your seating arrangements.

How do I change or cancel a request?

Call or WhatsApp 07768 489048, or email bookings@theboardgamecafe.co.uk. Include your name, visit date and arrival time so we can find your request. Any booking-specific terms will be confirmed with you.

What if I have a food allergy?

Speak to the team before ordering and tell us about your allergy or dietary requirement. We will check the relevant product information with you.

Groups & practical details

Can I book a birthday party?

Yes. Birthday parties can be arranged as group bookings or, where suitable, private venue hire. Party and group bookings start from £50 an hour.

Can I hire the whole venue?

Yes. Exclusive whole-venue hire is £100 per hour, with a three-hour minimum (£300), for up to 80 guests. A 50% deposit confirms the booking, and cancellations must be made at least 24 hours before the event. Food and refreshments are charged separately; bringing outside food costs £50. Dates, times, layout and the full quote are confirmed before booking.

Can businesses or schools book?

Yes. Corporate socials, workshops, school and college group visits and organised activities can all be discussed with us.

Is there parking?

Free parking is available on site at The Stables, including accessible parking. Follow local signage and any restrictions in place when you visit.

Is The Board Game Café family friendly?

Yes. Families and children are welcome. Board games, consoles, desserts and relaxed seating make it easy for families to enjoy the venue together. For later evening visits with younger children, contact us if you want to check a particular time or event.

Do you have Wi-Fi?

Yes. Free Wi-Fi is available for customers.

Can you arrange catering for groups?

Yes. Pre-arranged catering and group food/drink options can be discussed when you enquire.

Can we use the venue screens for an event?

For suitable group and private bookings, ask us about using the venue TVs for presentations, branded content or event material. Share what you want to display so we can confirm the setup.

Can we bring our own food for private hire?

Yes. There is a £50 fee for bringing outside food. Kitchen use is available for guests; agree the arrangements with us before booking. We can also discuss catering, with food and refreshments charged separately.

Can we request hire outside normal opening hours?

Private hire can be requested for any day or time, subject to availability and our confirmation. Tell us your preferred date, start time and duration.

What if I have an access requirement?

The entrance is step-free at street level, with wheelchair access and accessible parking. There is no accessible toilet. Contact us before visiting if you need a particular seating layout or other support.

Still unsure?

Ask us directly.

If your question is specific to a group, fixture, event or access need, send it over and we’ll confirm the details.
