Plan your game

Get the group around one board

Decide which rules you’re using before anyone rolls, especially if your family usually plays with house rules. That leaves more time for bargains and fewer arguments about Free Parking.

Plan for up to four at the table

Our board-game tables seat up to four. If you’re visiting as a larger group, ask us about arranging more than one table.

Agree how long you’ll play

Monopoly can run long once properties start changing hands. If you have a fixed finish time, agree a stopping point before you begin.

Trade with a purpose

Collecting a complete colour set gives you a route to building houses. Keep some cash available for rent and think through what the other player gains from a trade.
